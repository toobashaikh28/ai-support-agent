# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timeframes

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-7 business days after the order ships.
Expedited shipping, available at checkout for an additional fee, delivers
within 1-2 business days. International shipping typically takes 7-21
business days depending on destination and customs processing, which is
outside our control.

Orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time on a business day are
typically processed and shipped the same day. Orders placed on weekends or
public holidays ship on the next business day.

## Shipping costs

Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping within the domestic
region. Orders under that threshold incur a flat shipping fee of $6.99 for
standard shipping. Expedited shipping costs $14.99 regardless of order
value. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on
destination and package weight.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed to the customer as soon as the order ships.
Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after the label is
created, since this reflects the time for the carrier to scan the package
into their system, not a shipping delay.

## Lost or delayed packages

If tracking shows no movement for more than 7 business days (domestic) or
21 business days (international), the customer should contact support to
open a carrier investigation before a replacement or refund is issued. We
do not issue automatic replacements for packages that are merely delayed,
only for packages confirmed lost by the carrier.

## Address changes

Shipping addresses can only be changed while an order status is "Pending."
Once an order has moved to "Paid" or later, the address cannot be changed
through support and the customer must contact the carrier directly, or
allow the package to be delivered and arrange a forwarding request.

## Damaged in transit

If a package arrives visibly damaged, the customer should refuse delivery
if possible, or contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of
the damage. Damaged-in-transit claims do not require a return of the
damaged item before a replacement or refund is issued.

## Multi-package orders

Orders containing items from different warehouses may arrive in separate
packages with separate tracking numbers, sometimes on different days. This
is not a partial shipment error; the order confirmation email lists each
package's expected delivery window separately when this applies. Customers
are not charged additional shipping for split packages resulting from
warehouse location, only for shipping method chosen at checkout.

## PO boxes and freight restrictions

Standard packages can be delivered to PO boxes, but items requiring signature
confirmation or oversized freight shipping (furniture, large appliances)
cannot be delivered to a PO box and require a physical street address. Orders
placed with a PO box address that contain a freight-restricted item will be
held and the customer contacted to provide an alternate address before the
order ships.

## Redelivery after failed delivery attempts

Most carriers attempt delivery up to three times before returning a package
to the local depot or, after a holding period, back to our warehouse. If a
package is returned to us as undeliverable, we contact the customer to
confirm the address and reship at no additional charge, or issue a full
refund if the customer prefers not to reship.

## Signature requirements

Orders over $300 require an adult signature on delivery by default. This
can be waived at checkout for domestic orders, but doing so shifts liability
for a lost or stolen package (confirmed delivered by tracking) to the
customer rather than qualifying for a replacement under this policy.
