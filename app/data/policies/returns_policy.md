# Returns Policy

## Eligibility

Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date, provided they are
unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase
(order number or receipt). Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout are not
eligible for return under any circumstances.

Opened software, digital downloads, gift cards, and personal care items
(including cosmetics and earrings) cannot be returned once the packaging
has been opened, for hygiene and licensing reasons.

## How to start a return

Customers can start a return from their order history by selecting the
order and choosing "Request Return." A prepaid return label is generated
automatically for domestic orders. International customers are responsible
for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

## Condition requirements

Returned items must include all original tags, accessories, and manuals.
Items returned without original packaging may be subject to a 15%
restocking fee, deducted from the refund amount. Items that show signs of
use beyond trying them on (for clothing) or basic inspection (for
electronics) may be rejected and sent back to the customer at their own
cost.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item for a different
size, color, or model, return the original item for a refund and place a
new order separately. This ensures the replacement item ships as quickly
as possible rather than waiting for the returned item to be received first.

## Processing time

Once a returned item arrives at our warehouse, inspection typically takes
2-3 business days. Customers receive an email confirmation once the return
has been inspected and approved, at which point the refund is issued
according to the Refunds Policy.
