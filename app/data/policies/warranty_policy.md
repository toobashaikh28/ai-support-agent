# Warranty Policy

## Standard coverage

All products purchased directly from us include a 12-month manufacturer
warranty from the date of delivery, covering defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use. This is separate from the 30-day Returns
Policy window and remains valid even after a return period has expired.

Electronics carry an additional 90-day "dead on arrival" (DOA) provision:
if a device fails to power on or function at all within 90 days of
delivery, it qualifies for a full replacement rather than a repair, with
no restocking fee.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, accidental drops, liquid
exposure, unauthorized repairs or modifications, normal wear and tear
(such as battery capacity loss over time), or cosmetic damage that does not
affect function. Products used for commercial or industrial purposes
outside their intended consumer use are covered only under a separate
commercial warranty, if purchased at checkout.

## Filing a warranty claim

Customers should contact support with their order number, a description of
the defect, and photos or video if the issue is visual or mechanical. Our
team will determine whether the product qualifies for repair, replacement,
or refund under warranty. Approved warranty claims do not require the
customer to pay return shipping, regardless of how much time has passed
since delivery, as long as it falls within the 12-month window.

## Repair vs. replacement

For defects covered under warranty after the 90-day DOA window, we
generally offer a repair first if one is available; if the product cannot
be repaired or a repair would take longer than 15 business days, a
replacement of the same or equivalent model is issued instead. Customers
are not offered a choice between the two if a repair is feasible and
timely.

## Extended warranty

An optional extended warranty can be purchased at checkout, extending
coverage to 24 or 36 months. Extended warranty terms mirror the standard
warranty's coverage exclusions; it only extends the duration, not what is
covered.

## Warranty transfers

The warranty is tied to the original order and is not transferable to a
third party if the product is resold or gifted, unless proof of the
original purchase is provided along with the new owner's details.

## Common questions

**Does a warranty claim reset the 12-month clock?** No. A replacement
issued under warranty carries the remainder of the original 12-month
period, not a fresh 12 months from the replacement date. This prevents
indefinite coverage through repeated claims on the same underlying defect.

**What if the product was a gift and I don't have the order number?**
We can look up the order using the gift-giver's email address or the
shipping address the product was sent to, provided the customer can
confirm the approximate purchase date and product name.

**Can I get a cash refund instead of a repair or replacement under
warranty?** Only if both repair and replacement are unavailable — for
example, the product has been discontinued and no equivalent model
exists. In that case a refund is issued for the original purchase price,
not the current market price of a comparable product.

**Does accidental damage from a manufacturing defect count as misuse?**
No. If a defect directly causes secondary damage — for example, a battery
defect that damages surrounding components — the resulting damage is
covered under the same warranty claim, provided the original defect can be
verified as a manufacturing issue rather than external damage.

**Is proof of purchase required for every claim?** Yes, though it does not
need to be the original receipt. Order confirmation emails, packing slips,
or a lookup by the account email on file are all accepted as proof of
purchase.
