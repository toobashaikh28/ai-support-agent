# Refunds Policy

## Refund method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used at
checkout. Card refunds typically appear on the customer's statement within
5-10 business days after we issue them, depending on the card issuer. Bank
transfer refunds can take up to 10 business days. Wallet payments (such as
store credit) are refunded instantly.

## Partial refunds

If only some items in an order are returned, the refund covers only the
price of the returned items, not the full order total. Shipping charges
from the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to our
error (wrong item shipped, item arrived damaged, or item was defective).

If a partial refund is issued while an order still shows other items as
pending delivery, the order status remains unchanged; only the payment
record reflects the partial refund amount.

## Cancelled orders

If an order is cancelled before it ships, the full amount is refunded
automatically within 24 hours, and no restocking fee applies since the item
was never sent. Orders can only be cancelled while their status is
"Pending" or "Paid" — once an order has shipped, it must go through the
standard Returns Policy instead of a cancellation.

## Refund disputes

If a customer believes a refund was calculated incorrectly, they can open
a support ticket referencing the order number. Refund amounts are
recalculated only if there is a clear discrepancy between the return
condition report and the amount issued; store credit is never substituted
for a card refund without the customer's explicit consent.

## Failed payments

If an order's payment failed at checkout, no charge was made and there is
nothing to refund. Customers should retry payment or contact support if a
failed payment appears to have been charged in error, since this typically
indicates a temporary authorization hold that will release automatically
within a few business days.
